## Home

- [Ausclimate Cool Seasons Dehumidifier](https://ausclimate.com.au/products/cool-seasons-premium-10l-desiccant-dehumidifier?srsltid=AfmBOook-TMhMcC2nQjVRArnPMF1SAgl223lSWyjH3uDc8dplCjF9Ntc) — This is a life saver particularly in the winter when the humidity is high. Mold grows at anywhere above 60% humidity and that is what the humidity is for basically the entire season. Any decent dehumidifier should do fine.
