## We let random organisms live inside of us
There seems to be a lot of people who think of the body not like a machine with inputs that we should know → leading to outputs that we understand ← but as an ecosystem. They speak of the gut as a wild environment that we can tame and feed but never fully control. They don’t say this with a frown however, this is a good thing to them. Some people love feeling as if we have some connection to nature in that way, or that there are just things that we cannot control or understand but must learn to live with.
This is, of course, not the case. It may be true that due to our still very primitive circumstances and ways of living, our bodies are a mess of bacteria, viruses, waste products and the like. But it need not be the case. We *can* and **should** understand our bodies better in an effort to overcome. To transcend into people that can operate outside of the coercion of the body – not to lose our senses or abilities that our physical bodies allow, but to control them. To choose what happens when, and why.
