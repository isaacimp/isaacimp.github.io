A small Mac menu bar app for tracking deep work. You can start a live timer or log sessions you already did, look at your work over time with heatmaps and charts, and jot down quick notes after each block.

The main thing I wanted was a handoff note between sessions. When you finish working on a project, you write a couple of lines about what you got done and where to pick up next time. The next time you open a timer for that project, those notes sit right at the top so you can get straight back into it.

## What it does

- Lives quietly in the Mac menu bar with a small, clean UI.
- Live timer (open-ended or target length) and quick manual logging for past work.
- Short summary of what you're working on, post-session notes, and "next time" handoff notes per project.
- Consistency heatmap, 7D / 30D / 12W charts, and project breakdown.
- Everything is stored locally as plain JSON, with optional CSV/JSON auto-export for custom dashboards and folder sync (iCloud Drive / Dropbox).
- Extra options live in Settings so the main view stays uncluttered: Pomodoro mode, extra charts (peak hours, day of week, session lengths), background themes, and accent colors.
