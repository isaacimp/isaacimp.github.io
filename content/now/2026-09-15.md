Ive been working on an archival/ tracking program based on [ark](https://jamierubin.net/ark/) by Jamie Rubin. It is a very personal program that allows me to archive anything that I would like to keep in a more seperate specific enviroment with a database to keep track of everything. This makes anything from pdfs to papers very searchable and accessible when needed. This has also allowed me to set up a bit of a life tracking system in there where I can track many things from my diet to the books im reading and even turn that data into charts or query it from the database. The big thing for me was also making a system where it is very easy to stick automations into it so I have designed it with that in mind and now it is very easy to create an automated tracker for emails or keys typed or anything and stick it in there to log each day into the database and become connected to everything else.
