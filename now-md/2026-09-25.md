## Arkiv
My arkiv software now tracks my every move. It knows my heart beat better then I. It sees my emails before I even know that they exist. It holds in it's memory every single bank transaction I have ever made.

*To be clear* I dont know if this is a good idea but it's something that I've been thinking about for years now and I finally have created a system that allows me to easily track a huge variety of things without me having to even think about it. I was worried (and still am) about the privacy aspect but then I remembered that the only reason I am able to get most of this data is becuase the device I am using to get it tracks and stores it somewhere anyway. I might as well store it safely myself as well and then I can also actually make use of it. I have made quite a few interesting design descisons here which I will go into more detail about in a dedicated format.

**Why I think this data is important:**

Obviously there is the novelty and fun of having this kind of data tracked over many years, but I wish to look beyond that.

Everything about us is physical, from our body to the brain in particular. When we understand the body and our biology better than we do now, we will be able to make highly accurate predictions based on a variety of metrics and data. We will not, on the other hand, be able to make these predictions about the output of our minds and creativity, as creativity is, by nature, unpredictable.

Preserving the mind is above all else, and for now, the best vessel for the mind is our wet brain and body. So, the importance comes to preserving those. I think true understanding and forming good explanations of those is above all else when it comes to solving our health and aging issues, but data is very useful in applying and testing those ideas so as long as it remains easy to do, I will continue to track.
