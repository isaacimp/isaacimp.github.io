I'm a developer and builder in Melbourne. I design health-tracking hardware under the name at1a, write about diet science and epistemology, and keep most of what I make and think here — mostly so I don't lose track of it myself.

Info

I split my time between a part-time courier shift and everything else — firmware, enclosure design, and whatever's broken my sleep tracker this week. My guiding framework is Deutschian: good explanations are hard to vary, problems are always solvable, and the interesting work is usually the work nobody's asked for yet.

Currently building at1a v0.3, mostly fighting the enclosure fit. Reading The Beginning of Infinity. Stack is C, Odin, Python and Rust. On GitHub and YouTube. Melbourne time, and I reply the same day.

more about me

I've got celiac disease, which is most of why the diet-science posts exist — a lot of it started as figuring out my own gut rather than idle curiosity. Outside of that: gold prospecting around Victoria on weekends, way too much time spent on Neovim config, and a small collection of old scientific texts including an original 1686 Philosophical Transactions.
