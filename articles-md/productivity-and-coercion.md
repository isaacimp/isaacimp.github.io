I have arrived, after much thought, at the conclusion that coercion and related ailments are some of the most damaging things for creativity and productivity

## Coercion is the enemy of progress
The kind of coercion that I'm talking about here is the coercion that we inflict upon ourselves. Self coercion is basically everywhere, especially in the self help/ productivity circles as those ideas create a sort of breeding ground for coercive ideas to spread.

> Discipline is fighting yourself. - Lulie Tanett

The idea of Discipline as it is usually talked about is a state where there is constant conflict in ones mind and we are expected according to the tenents of so called "productivity" to ignore the conflict and just do the thing even if every step feels like we are forcing ourselves to walk on hot coals.
