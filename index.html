The fragility of our biology

I used to think that due to the many years of out evolution and through the many self correcting and highly refined processes of our biology that we were very anti-fragile and it was amazing how out bodies could seem to self correct and perform amazing feats to keep us alive. I don't think that anymore. Let me explain.

I concede that our bodies are able to (and do) amazing things that really do keep us alive and I will likely talk more specifically about some of those things. The issue I have is that a lot of this is thought and talked about in very relative frames of reference where we think it's amazing because we are unable to replicate it or becuase we can leverage it to live 50% longer or aid our systems in removing disease or viruses. All of that is fantastic but if the only goal with our health is to remove disease and help us all to live to 100, that jsut doesn't cut it for me.
