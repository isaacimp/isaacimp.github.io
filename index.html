I haven't updated my now page recently since I was unwell for a while and did not really have the energy to write about much and not too much has changed for me anyway.

I have been working and saving for my trip in less then two weeks and I am preparing for that. One of the ways I am preparing is getting a few of my own personal ideas and problems in order because everytime I do something big like this I like to go into it ready to make some change and make the most of it.

The most notable change I made recently in regards to productivity type stuff is switching to a small physical notebook for my general notetaking. I have mine set up a bit like Tim Ferris does here with an index and so on.

I made this change for a couple main reasons.

  1. I realised that for now I actually prefer a paper notebook for the general daily basic notetaking that I do.
  2. I would like to reduce the perceived import of general notetaking. It should be treated more so as an extention of memory then any kind of real storage or reference material. I would prefer to structure my life around creative problem solving and projects. This means that when I am working on something larger and real like an article or a video or even just a problem that I wish to solve, I can create a specfic environment (probably digital) where all of that work and content can go to in that one place (and of course altered and improved over time). This allows notes to remain the informal tool that they are just to help the thinking process and get ideas down but not as a place to store real finished ideas. That is where the digital can come in.
