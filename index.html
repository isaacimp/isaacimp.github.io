Potato

I wish to write about the wonderful Potato. No matter what I do I cannot escape the Potato. Everything I learn about them makes me more and more fascinated with how this one vegetable can provide so much nutrition with so little downsides. I have yet to find a perfect food that meets my criteria and I don’t think there is such thing as a perfect naturally occurring food but the Potato is very high up on the list.

First of all, the nutritional content of a good potato is outstanding. The potato provides meaningful amounts of a remarkably wide range of nutrients. It contains all essential amino acids, excellent vitamin C and B6, substantial potassium and other minerals. While potatoes don't contain every vitamin (they're missing B12, A, D, and E like all plant foods), they come closer to nutritional completeness than nearly any other single staple food.
